  • Coverage depends on third-party network operators in each destination, and can change when an operator changes its wholesale terms, its technology or its roaming arrangements.
  • Technology tiers (3G, 4G and 5G) and speeds vary by market and are not guaranteed. The technology shown for a country is the highest generally available on the networks we use; the actual technology your end user attaches to depends on the handset, the band support, the location and network load. We do not guarantee any particular speed, latency or throughput.
  • Service may be interrupted by scheduled maintenance, network faults, capacity constraints, or events outside our reasonable control, including outages affecting an operator, a provisioning provider or our cloud infrastructure.
  • Permanent-roaming and fair-use restrictions apply in some markets. Regulators and operators in a number of countries restrict permanent roaming or impose fair-use monitoring. Examples include Turkey, Brazil, India and China, and the EU fair-use monitoring window that applies to roam-like-at-home arrangements. Where these restrictions apply, a profile may be suspended, throttled or required to re-register after a period of continuous use, and we will tell you which destinations are affected before you sell there.

We will give you as much warning as we reasonably can when a change to coverage or availability affects a destination you are selling, and we will confirm the affected SKUs so that you can adjust your own offer.

As the partner facing the end user, you are responsible for:

  • obtaining and providing to us any identity information required by local SIM-registration law in the territories into which you sell, in the form and to the standard that the destination market requires, and doing so lawfully;
  • making your own consumer-facing disclosures — telling your end users what data you collect, why, who it is shared with, including the network operator or regulator in the destination, and how long it is kept;
  • obtaining any consent or authorisation your own customers' law requires before you pass their data to us or to an operator, and answering your customers' questions about that data; and

We will tell you which of your destinations require registration, what has to be collected, and how it has to be passed to us, and we will pass it to the operator or regulator as required. We may have to suspend a profile where a registration obligation is not met, because in those markets the operator will do so in any event.

10. Refunds, returns and unused eSIM profiles

Refund handling follows the state of the profile, because our cost with the network operator follows the same line.

  • Unused eSIM profiles. An unused profile can be cancelled and credited to your partner account balance before activation. Until a profile is activated, no data cost has been incurred with the operator, so it is returned to your balance rather than lost. Requests should identify the ICCIDs or the order reference.
  • Activated profiles. Once a profile has been activated, refunds are assessed case by case, because the data cost has already been incurred with the network operator and, in many cases, cannot be recovered from them. We look at what was actually consumed, what the operator will credit, and whether the failure was on our side — for example a provisioning or activation fault, which we will make good.
  • Refunds to end customers. Where an end customer is to be refunded, the refund is returned through the original payment method where the payment provider supports it. Where it does not, we will agree an alternative with you in writing.
  • Physical stock. Physical SIM cards that are unsold and unopened may be returned with our prior authorisation. We will authorise the return and issue a collection address, and we credit your account once the goods arrive and are confirmed to be in resalable condition. Cards that have been activated, defaced or opened cannot be credited. Return shipping is arranged as agreed in the partner agreement.
