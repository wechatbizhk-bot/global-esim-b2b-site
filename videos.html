The eSIM advantage

Why eSIM wins — commercially, not just technically

Anyone can list the technical benefits. These are the eight that actually change a travel seller's numbers.

No inventory, no logistics, no customs

Nothing is manufactured, warehoused or shipped. That removes working capital, storage cost, freight and customs exposure from your P&L in a single decision.

Delivered in seconds, anywhere

A profile is provisioned on demand and delivered by QR code or a one-tap install link. A customer can buy at the departure gate and still be connected on landing.

A fraction of the roaming cost

Travel eSIMs are roughly 75% cheaper per GB than traditional roaming by 2029 (Juniper Research). You can price well below the roaming alternative and still hold a healthy margin.

One device, several profiles

The traveller keeps their home number active for calls and messages while the data runs on your plan — no SIM tray, no swapping, no losing the original card.

A compliance advantage, not a burden

Over 155 countries now require SIM registration. A data-only travel profile carries no local number and so usually falls outside those regimes — a genuine regulatory simplification.

Built for corporate control

Usage visibility, consolidated billing and a top-up window of up to twelve months mean a travelling workforce stops filing per-trip expense claims for data.

Replaceable in seconds

A lost or broken handset does not mean a lost plan. The profile is re-issued to the new device — no waiting for a replacement card to arrive in the post.

Repeat revenue at no acquisition cost

An eSIM can be topped up for up to twelve months after its last use. The second sale to the same traveller costs you nothing to acquire — which is where channel economics improve sharply.

Video ideas that actually sell

If you are producing content, these are the four that move a B2B conversation forward:

  • A 60-second activation. Film a phone going from QR code to connected. Speed is the single most persuasive thing you can show.
  • A white-label unboxing. Your partner's brand on the card, the jacket and the handset network display.
  • A cost comparison. One traveller, one week, roaming bill versus your bundle. Put the numbers on screen.
  • A console walkthrough. Order creation, ICCID lookup and usage, filmed end to end.
