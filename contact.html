What to include in your message

If your first message covers these six points, we can usually come back with a usable number instead of asking you to fill in a form.

  • Destinations or regions. Country by country if you know them, or a region if you do not — "Southeast Asia plus Japan" is a perfectly good starting point.
  • Expected monthly volume. Users or cards per month, and say whether that is a pilot figure or a steady-state figure. A pilot of 500 and a production run of 50,000 sit in different pricing tiers.
  • eSIM, physical SIM or both. eSIM needs no inventory and no shipping; physical cards carry a per-unit cost and a lead time, and many partners run both for different customer segments.
  • Whether you need white label. Your brand on the handset's network display, your artwork and your packaging, or our standard profile through your own API integration.
  • Your target launch date. Onboarding is typically two to four weeks, and physical card production adds to that. If the date is fixed, say so early — it changes what we recommend.
  • Whether a pilot is required. If your procurement process needs a trial first, tell us and we will structure the first order as a paid or test-credit pilot.
