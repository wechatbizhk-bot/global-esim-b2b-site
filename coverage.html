How to read these rates

What the number actually means

The figure shown against a country is the lowest indicative partner rate across all of the listed network partners for that country, in HKD per GB. It is a floor, not an average, and not a retail price.

Rates are set per country and per network partner, and the technology tier behind them varies by market. Two partners in the same country can sit on different technologies and different rates — a 5G partner and a 3G partner are not interchangeable products, even though both appear under one country row.

The technology badges show what is available in the market, not what every partner runs. Where a market shows 3G only, the wholesale cost of moving that market to 4G or 5G is a commercial conversation, not a setting.

The full per-network rate card is released under NDA after partner verification. That card breaks the country figure into each individual network partner, with the rate, the technology tier and the available bundle sizes for each.

Volume tiers reduce the per-unit SIM price as order quantity grows, from HK$5 per card at 500–4,999 pcs down to HK$3 at 20,000+ pcs. 30 days' written notice is given before any cost or offer change.

Indicative figures, not a quotation

These figures are indicative, published for orientation only. A binding quotation is issued after network availability and volume pricing are confirmed. Rates move with wholesale cost, currency and local network conditions, so nothing on this page is an offer capable of acceptance.

Why publish a floor rate at all

Most wholesale eSIM pricing is only revealed after a sales call. Publishing the floor lets you sanity-check whether a market is viable for your product before either side spends time on it.

If a market looks expensive at the floor rate, the honest answer is usually that it is expensive — not that a discount is waiting.

Designing your bundle

From a per-GB rate to a retail product

Three decisions turn a wholesale rate into something your customer can buy. All three are yours to make.

Choose the destination set

Pick the markets your customers actually travel to. A single-country bundle, a regional bundle or a multi-region bundle each price differently, because the rate is drawn from the most expensive market in the set.

Choose the bundle size

Standard sizes are 1, 3, 5, 10, 20 and 50 GB. Data cost is simply the per-GB rate multiplied by the size, so a bigger bundle carries a bigger absolute cost but the same rate per GB.

Apply your own markup

The retail price is your decision, not ours. Add a physical SIM cost if you are shipping cards: HK$5, HK$4 or HK$3 per card depending on order quantity.

What the example shows

A 5 GB Singapore bundle costs HK$40 in data. If you sell it at HK$98, your gross margin before payment fees is HK$58. If you ship a physical card at the HK$5 tier, the same bundle costs HK$45 and the margin is HK$53.

A 5 GB Europe bundle at the HK$40 / GB regional rate costs HK$200 in data, so the retail price has to be materially higher — or the bundle smaller — for the same percentage margin.
