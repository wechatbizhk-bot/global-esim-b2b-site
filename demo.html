    What we will show you in 45 minutes

    Six things, in this order. If you only care about one of them, tell us and we will spend the whole session on it.

    1. Your destinations, live

    Send us your top ten markets beforehand, or just name them in the call. We will walk them against our live coverage and per-network rate card while you watch.

    2. An eSIM provisioned end to end

    A profile issued and activated on a real handset, start to finish, so you can see how fast it actually is rather than taking our word for it.

    3. The partner console

    Order creation, ICCID lookup, usage retrieval and activation status — the operational reality your team would work with every day.

    4. A white-label sample

    SIM artwork and packaging presented under your own brand, so you can judge whether it meets your design standards before you commit.

    5. A costed margin model

    Your destinations, your bundle sizes, your volumes — and the resulting contribution, with every assumption stated openly so your finance team can challenge it.

    6. A pilot scope — or a straight answer

    By the end of the session we will either agree a pilot outline or tell you plainly that we are not the right fit. Both are useful outcomes.

    What we need from you

    One commercial contact and one technical contact. Nothing in advance, no preparation, and no obligation. If you would rather bring four people, bring four.
