How this order is priced

The blended rate is the average of the lowest indicative partner rate across the destinations you selected, in HKD per gigabyte. Data cost is that rate multiplied by the bundle size and the quantity. SIM card pricing follows the volume tiers: HK$5 per card from 500–4,999 pieces, HK$4 from 5,000–19,999, and HK$3 from 20,000 upwards. A partner deposit from HK$20,000 applies and is credited against your first orders. Every figure here is indicative; your signed partner agreement is definitive.
